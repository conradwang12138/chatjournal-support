Find notes and drafts

Use Browse notes, Search or Date from the main screen. For a note deleted by mistake, open “Recently deleted” and choose “Restore”. A permanently deleted note cannot be restored from that page.

To find a draft or earlier content, open “Settings → Backup & import → Drafts & versions”. An ordinary note's menu also offers “Versions”. Restoring historical text and attachments retains the current content as another historical version. It does not resolve pending category changes on your behalf.

Use Auto-organize

Auto-organize is off by default. Enabling it in Settings first checks available memory. On first use, confirm a 2.74 GB (2,740,937,888-byte) model download from Hugging Face and its delivery network; internet access and free storage are required. The download service receives connection information such as your IP address, without note text or attachments. No model account or key is required. After verification, the model organizes saved, visible text on your device, including existing notes. Original text and attachments are saved first and are not uploaded to the developer or model provider.

You can cancel a download. To try again after cancelling, turn Auto-organize on and confirm the download. If a failed download shows “Retry”, choose it. A saved partial resumes when it can be safely matched; otherwise the download starts again. Cancellation or failure does not enable organization. “Remove Model” in Settings stops organization and removes the model files while retaining notes and attachments. You can download it again later. If available memory is insufficient or cannot be confirmed, you can keep taking notes and retry later.

Auto-organize may add categories and existing tags, and extract to-dos from explicit, unfinished actions. It does not add to-dos to every older note: older notes receive category organization by default, while new notes may be marked as to-dos based on their actions. You can change categories, tags or to-dos manually. It processes text only, without directly analyzing photos or audio; recognized or transcribed text you choose to add to a note can also be organized. It does not reply to notes or create notifications automatically.

If you see “Organization did not finish. Your notes are saved. Try again.”, “Organization paused because memory is low. Your notes are saved.” or a message that a note is too long, your saved notes and attachments remain available. You can keep taking notes and use “Retry” later. Avoid repeatedly importing or recreating notes to trigger organization. Turning Auto-organize off stops new organization and keeps saved results.

Notes-and-attachments packages, note backups and original-files backups can retain related organization data, including quoted action text. A readable Markdown or plain-text export does not contain all organization metadata. If you separately enable iCloud sync, that data may sync with your notes. See the Privacy Policy.

Back up, export and import

Open “Settings → Backup & import”. For a complete recovery copy, choose “Back up all notes”. It includes hidden and recently deleted content, history, original attachments, drafts and appearance settings. For portable copies of selected notes, use “Export notes” and choose “Notes and attachments”, Markdown or plain text as needed.

Use “Import notes or backup” to select a file, review its contents, then confirm. Keep attachment packages intact. An original-files backup preserves the underlying storage files; restoring one first creates a separate copy rather than overwriting the current library.

Backups and exports do not add encryption. Keep independent copies and check for hidden, recently deleted or historical content before sharing. See the Privacy Policy.

Camera, recording, text recognition or transcription is unavailable

Check camera, microphone and speech recognition permissions in system Settings. If speech permission was denied, use the app’s “Open Settings” control to open system Settings. Declining optional permissions still allows ordinary text notes and available file imports.

Text recognition runs on the device. Transcription depends on local Speech support for your device, system and language. It does not fall back to cloud transcription. You can still keep or export the original audio. Large files may take time; avoid repeatedly starting the same import.

Sync and networking

“Settings → iCloud → Sync notes” is off by default. Before enabling it, check that the device is signed in to your Apple account, iCloud has enough space and a network connection is available. Notes already saved locally remain there while sync is waiting or fails. Check the status, then use “Sync now”. Enable sync again after an account change.

Turning sync off does not remove existing cloud copies. Avoid deleting the app or repeatedly importing a backup to diagnose sync, because that can add copies. Keep an independent backup and contact support first. Service and other-device availability depend on the system and network.

“Online previews” is off by default. It controls webpage previews fetched by the app. Links you open, or imports from cloud photos and file providers, may still use the network.

Hiding and permanent deletion

Hiding is not encryption. “Delete permanently” removes the app's restoration path; it does not guarantee secure erasure of historical files, original attachments, backups or cloud copies. Back up first if you need to keep a note. To remove all copies, also check places where you saved or shared them.
