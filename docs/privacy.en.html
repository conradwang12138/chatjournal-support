ChatJournal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-15

Developer: Ziheng Wang. Contact: wzh925704928@gmail.com.

Where your notes are stored

Text, photos, video, audio, drafts and manual organization data or Auto-organize results are stored in the app's library on your device by default. Ordinary note-taking does not require a ChatJournal account. The developer does not operate a server that receives journal content and does not upload your notes to a model provider. The app does not provide advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

The share extension stores content you choose to share on the device for the main app to receive. “Show note content” for widgets is off by default. If you enable it, widgets may display notes that meet the app's visibility rules on your Home Screen. Consider who can see your device before enabling this option.

Optional Auto-organize

Auto-organize is off by default. Before first use, the app shows the model size and asks for confirmation to download it from Hugging Face and its delivery network. That download provides the service with necessary network information, such as your IP address, without note text or attachments. After download and verification, the model organizes the text of saved, visible notes on your device, including existing notes and notes saved later. Original text and attachments are saved first. Organization does not rewrite them or upload note text or attachments to the developer or model provider.

The model asset is stored separately inside the app and excluded from backup and journal synchronization. You can remove it in Settings. Removing it turns off Auto-organize and keeps original notes and attachments. You can download it again later.

Auto-organize processes text only; it does not directly analyze photos, video or audio. Recognized or transcribed text you choose to add to a note can be organized like other note text. It may add categories and existing tags, and extract to-dos from explicit, unfinished actions. It does not automatically add to-dos to every existing note. Older notes receive category organization by default; new notes may be marked as to-dos based on their actions. You can edit organization results manually. It does not provide AI replies or questions and answers, or create system notifications automatically.

Turning it off stops new organization and keeps saved results. If organization pauses, fails or cannot process a long note, your saved original text and attachments remain available, and you can retry later. Results and related metadata are stored in the library and may include quoted action text and time expressions. They may be retained in complete record packages, backups and the iCloud sync you separately enable, as described below.

Optional iCloud sync

“Sync notes” is off by default. When you choose to enable it, notes, organization results and related metadata, version history, related original attachments and recovery data are transferred through Apple's iCloud service and use your iCloud storage. This includes hidden and recently deleted notes and their history. Hiding a note does not prevent it from syncing.

Turning sync off stops subsequent app sync; it does not automatically delete existing iCloud copies or copies on other devices, in system backups or in exports. Historical files and attachments may remain in the cloud. Apple provides the account, iCloud storage, backup and deletion controls through its systems and services. After an account change, the app requires you to enable sync again.

Websites, online previews and file providers

“Online previews” is off by default. If enabled, the app may request webpage information for links in your notes. Opening a link also contacts its website. Websites and network services may receive the requested address, your IP address and standard network information under their own policies. Avoid enabling previews for sensitive private links.

When using the system photo or file picker, the content you choose may be downloaded from iCloud Photos or another file provider. Copies exported or shared to another app, file location or cloud drive are managed by you and the relevant service.

On-device text recognition and transcription

Image text recognition uses Apple Vision on the device. Audio transcription is offered only where the device, system and language support local Speech recognition, and the app requires on-device recognition. If that is unavailable, transcription is unavailable; it does not fall back to a developer-operated or third-party cloud transcription service. These features do not provide AI conversations or replies.

Optional permissions

Camera access is for photos or scans you choose to capture. Microphone access is for recording. Speech recognition permission is used when you request audio transcription. Photo and file pickers import content you select. Notifications are for reminders you set. Manage these permissions in system Settings. Declining optional permissions does not block ordinary text notes.

Hiding, deletion and historical attachments

Hiding changes in-app visibility; it does not encrypt original files. It is not password protection or secure erasure.

“Delete” moves a note to “Recently deleted” and retains its text, attachments and related history so you can restore it. The app does not automatically empty Recently deleted after a fixed number of days.

“Delete permanently” prevents restoration through the app's Recently deleted page and excludes the note from subsequently generated structured note backups. It does not securely erase underlying files. Earlier text or attachments may remain in historical commits or attachment files in the local library, earlier backups, exports or iCloud copies.

When you edit text or remove an attachment from a note, earlier versions and original attachments may be retained for version recovery or because another note still uses them. Deleting or hiding a note does not automatically remove copies you saved, shared or placed in other services. If you need to remove every copy, also check your device, system backups, export destinations and cloud services. Do not treat the app's permanent-deletion action as confirmation that every copy has been erased.

Backups and exports

Ordinary exports use the visible-note scope you choose. “Notes and attachments” includes those notes, their original attachments and organization metadata, including quoted action text. A readable Markdown or plain-text export does not contain all organization metadata. Choose the format that retains what you need.

“Back up all notes” includes notes, version history, organization results and related metadata, original attachments, drafts and appearance settings, including hidden and recently deleted content. Permanently deleted notes are excluded from new structured backups.

“Original files backup” preserves the library's original storage files. It may include older versions, historical files of permanently deleted notes and retained attachments. Backups and exports do not add encryption. Store and share them carefully. Removing the app does not remove independent exports, backups, attachments you sent or cloud copies on your behalf.

Support email and this website

If you email support, your address, message and optional attachments are used to handle your request. Do not include unnecessary journal entries or sensitive information. Contact the same email to request deletion of support correspondence. Email providers also process messages under their own service rules.

This website loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts or third-party fonts. Its static hosting provider may still record basic access logs such as IP address, request path and time to operate and protect the website.
